The European Commission's Interoperability Portal is a platform dedicated to the interoperability of European digital public services.
The dashboard displays a catalogue of knowledge, standards, tools, solutions, and experiences related to the interoperability and digitalization of European public administrations.
The information shown in the chart is categorized according to the following criteria:
  • Digital government
  • Economics
  • Education
  • Energy and environment
  • Health
  • ICT
  • Innovation
